# Additional Notes — AI SEO Research

## Observations

* Most experts agree that AI is a tool, not a full solution
* Content quality and intent remain critical ranking factors
* AI is mainly used for scaling and ideation

## Patterns Identified

* Shift from keyword stuffing → topical authority
* Increased focus on user intent
* Integration of AI into existing SEO workflows

## Early Conclusion

AI-powered SEO is not about replacing human work, but enhancing it through faster execution and better data processing.
